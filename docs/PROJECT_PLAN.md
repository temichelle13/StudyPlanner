# StudyPlanner Project Plan

## Vision
Empower every learner—especially adult and non-traditional students—with a unified planning hub that combines academic scheduling with wraparound support. StudyPlanner will provide proactive financial aid guidance, scholarship discovery, prior-learning credit optimization, and coaching tailored to adult learners so that each person can navigate their educational journey with clarity, confidence, and sustainable funding.

## Objectives
- Deliver an integrated planning experience that maps coursework, degree requirements, and key milestones into personalized, adaptable learning pathways.
- Embed guidance for financial aid applications, scholarship matching, and funding renewal requirements directly into learner workflows.
- Surface prior-learning assessments and credit-transfer recommendations that shorten time-to-completion without sacrificing academic rigor.
- Provide adult-learner coaching resources, nudges, and office-hour style support that address confidence, time management, and work-life balance.
- Equip advisors with collaborative tools and analytics that highlight at-risk learners and opportunities for timely intervention.

## Goals
### Knowledge Base & Resource Curation
- Build and maintain a dynamic repository of financial aid guides, scholarship databases, prior-learning evaluation rubrics, and adult-learner success playbooks.
- Implement governance for content freshness, with quarterly reviews by subject-matter experts and advisor feedback loops.

### Advising & Guidance Workflows
- Design configurable advisor dashboards that surface learner readiness, funding status, and credit-optimization suggestions in real time.
- Automate nudges and follow-up tasks that align advisors, success coaches, and learners around upcoming deadlines and milestones.

### Partnerships & External Support Ecosystem
- Formalize referral pathways with community organizations, workforce agencies, childcare providers, and financial counseling services.
- Integrate trusted third-party tools (FAFSA helpers, scholarship search APIs, prior-learning assessment partners) via secure data exchanges and clear consent flows.

### Scheduling & Productivity Experience
- Offer drag-and-drop schedule builders that coordinate classes, work shifts, caregiving responsibilities, and study blocks.
- Provide AI-assisted recommendations that reconcile degree plans with available funding windows and transfer-credit opportunities.

## Success Criteria
- 85% of active learners create or refresh a personalized multi-term learning pathway that blends scheduling, funding strategy, and skill milestones within their first month.
- 70% of learners who engage with financial guidance secure new aid, scholarships, or payment plans, reducing out-of-pocket costs by an average of 15% per term.
- Prior-learning credit recommendations eliminate at least 1.5 courses per learner on average, shortening time-to-completion by one semester for adult learners.
- Advisors log interactions for 90% of assigned learners each term, with automated workflows covering follow-ups and external referrals within two business days.
- Calendar synchronization maintains a scheduling conflict rate below 2%, with notification accuracy validated through quarterly user satisfaction surveys scoring 4.3/5 or higher.
